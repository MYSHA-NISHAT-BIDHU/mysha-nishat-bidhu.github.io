# RT-DASH — Workload- and Event-Adaptive Dual-Mode Scheduling

**Status:** Published research (2026)  
**Researcher:** Mysha Nishat Bidhu; journal co-author: Jalil Boudjadar  
**Paper:** [Workload- and Event-Adaptive Dual-Mode Scheduling with Recovery-Aware Service Control for Smart-Home Controllers](https://doi.org/10.3390/a19100863), *Algorithms*, 19(10), 863 (2026)

## Research overview

RT-DASH investigates adaptive real-time scheduling for a centralized smart-home controller under changing resource pressure and safety-critical events. It combines Normal and Critical operation, switching according to workload conditions and event criticality, with controlled reductions in lower-criticality service and an explicit return-to-normal policy.

## Research components

- Deadline-aware scheduling with criticality-sensitive dispatch
- Workload- and event-triggered dual-mode operation
- Minimum-service protection for degradable tasks
- Resource-demand accounting and backlog-recovery evaluation
- Software simulation and reproducibility checks

## Evidence and scope

The published work reports **software-simulation** results, not verified worst-case timing on deployed hardware. The paper and its supplementary materials are the authoritative references for experiments, methods, and reproducibility.
